Privacy Policy — Sambo Bot

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information Sambo Bot ("the Bot") collects and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

  • Discord user ID — to link game progress to your account.
  • Game data — such as your coins, items, animals, weapons, levels, statistics, settings, cooldown timers, and clan membership.
  • Clan data — clan name, owner and member user IDs, and clan statistics.
  • Technical logs — error and status logs kept on our server to fix problems.

2. Message Content

The Bot reads the content of messages in channels it can see only to detect commands that start with its prefix ("s" followed by a command name). We do not store, sell, or share the content of your messages. Messages that are not commands are ignored.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information only to run the Bot: saving your progress, showing your stats, leaderboards, and clan information, preventing abuse, and fixing errors. Usernames shown on leaderboards are retrieved from Discord at display time.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your information. Data is not shared with third parties, except as required by law or to enforce our Terms of Service. The Bot communicates with Discord's API, which is governed by Discord's own Privacy Policy.

5. Storage and Security

Data is stored on a server controlled by the Bot's operator. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no system is completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Game data is kept while the Bot is in use. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us (see below); we will delete it within a reasonable time. Removing the Bot from a server does not by itself delete your user game data.

7. Children

The Bot is intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirement. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone below that age. If you believe this has happened, contact us and we will delete the data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
